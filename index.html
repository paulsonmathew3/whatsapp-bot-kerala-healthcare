Demo guide

This simulates the proposed WhatsApp journey. No patient or payment data is sent anywhere.

  1. Select a language and service.
  2. For a valid sample patient, enter 1234567890123456.
  3. Try booking, cancelling, rescheduling, or viewing medicines.
In production, buttons and lists become WhatsApp Interactive Messages; the booking sequence becomes a Meta WhatsApp Flow.

What this demonstrates

  • 16-digit UHID validation and three-attempt safety limit
  • District → hospital → department → doctor → slot selection
  • Appointment confirmation with a demo token and QR-like ticket
  • Read-only medicine details and a return-to-menu route
